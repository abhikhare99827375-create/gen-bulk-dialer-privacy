Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

Gen Bulk Dialer ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed, used, stored, and shared when you use the Gen Bulk Dialer mobile application.

1. Information We Access

Depending on the features you use, Gen Bulk Dialer may access the following information:

Account Information

If you use Google Sign-In, we use Google and Firebase Authentication to authenticate your account. This may provide information such as your name, email address, profile information, and authentication identifier.

We use this information to provide and maintain your account and authentication functionality.

Contacts

If you grant the required permission, the app may access contacts stored on your device.

Contacts may be used to allow you to select, organize, and manage phone numbers for calling campaigns.

The app does not sell your contact information.

Imported Files

The app may allow you to import contact lists or calling data from supported Excel or CSV files.

Imported information is used to provide the application's contact and campaign-management features.

Call and Phone Functionality

The app may use Android phone and calling functionality to allow you to place calls to numbers selected by you.

Gen Bulk Dialer does not independently record or listen to the content of your phone conversations.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed through the app may be used to:

  • Provide Google account authentication.
  • Create and manage your account.
  • Import and organize contacts.
  • Create and manage calling campaigns.
  • Allow you to initiate phone calls.
  • Provide and maintain app functionality.
  • Troubleshoot technical problems and improve the application.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Google Sign-In and Firebase

Gen Bulk Dialer uses Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In for account authentication.

When you choose to sign in with Google, authentication is handled using Google's authentication services and Firebase.

Information processed by these services is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

4. Device Permissions

The application may request permissions required for features you choose to use, including permissions related to:

  • Contacts
  • Phone and calling functionality
  • Access to files or documents for importing supported contact lists

You can manage application permissions through your Android device settings.

5. Data Storage

Some application data, such as campaign information and imported contact information, may be stored locally on your device to provide the application's functionality.

Authentication-related information may be processed through Firebase services.

The exact information stored depends on the features you use.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the application, including authentication and infrastructure providers such as Google Firebase.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or governmental authority.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the application.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Gen Bulk Dialer is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application without appropriate permission, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

You can:

  • Deny or revoke device permissions through Android settings.
  • Stop using the application at any time.
  • Sign out of your account through the application.

  • Contact us regarding privacy-related questions or requests.
